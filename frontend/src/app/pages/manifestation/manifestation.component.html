We believe in architecture as a silent framework for light, shadow, and raw geometry. Our practice is an ongoing research into structural reduction and spatial clarity.

In a world cluttered with visual noise, we seek the power of the unfinished, the silent, and the void. The floating cube field represents our view of space: structured yet fragmented, stable yet light. Every block is a potential, defined not by its mass, but by the volume it displaces and the light it captures.

Our projects do not impose themselves on the context; rather, they form dialogues. Through careful manipulation of raw materials—concrete, glass, timber, and steel—we construct spaces that change continuously with the path of the sun. The building becomes an instrument for recording time.

"Space is not the absence of matter, but the presence of light."

We do not design buildings; we outline coordinates. We define boundaries so that the atmosphere inside can emerge naturally. It is in the tension between weight and weightlessness, opacity and transparency, that our architecture finds its home.
